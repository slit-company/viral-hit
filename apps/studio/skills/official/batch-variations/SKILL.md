# Make variations in parallel

Use when a request names two or more outputs, characters, hooks, languages or formats, even if the user never says "batch".

1. **Count.** State the number of outputs and what varies. Two lists do not multiply into every combination unless asked. "Make lots" without a number needs a count or a priced one-item pilot first.
2. **Share preparation.** Do character setup, reference imports, scripts and model choice once. Reuse one approved creative plan across items.
3. **Separate dependencies.** Independent items run side by side. A chain (image, then approval, then video of that image) stays ordered within its item; other items do not wait for it. Caption or language variants of one finished video reuse that video instead of generating a new one.
4. **Price the whole batch.** Price one item, multiply, show the total and get approval. Approval of a budget does not approve unseen stills that need review.
5. **Submit every ready item before waiting on any.** Ready means inputs, estimate, approval and required reviews are complete. Keep each item's job and intent token with the item.
6. **Account for every item.** Finish with a list: done, failed (with the real error), running or blocked (with the reason). A successful sibling does not prove the batch is complete. Name outputs by input order (`_v01`, `_v02`), never by finish order.

Respect real service quotas; do not invent a cap the service does not impose.
