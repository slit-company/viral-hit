Viral Hit is local-first software. The project maintainers do not operate a service that receives your projects, and the software has no analytics or telemetry.

## Data kept by your installation

Your installation stores projects, Canvas and timeline documents, media files, generation and job receipts, publication records and settings in its own data directory. Provider secrets and OAuth tokens are encrypted with a key held by that installation. Unsaved timeline drafts stay in your browser's local storage.

## Data sent to services you connect

When you run an action that needs a connected service, Viral Hit sends only what that action requires:

- Generations send prompts, settings and selected reference media to your configured Cloud API, ComfyUI or ima2 server.
- Reference intake downloads the video you selected from YouTube or Instagram.
- An approved publication uploads the reviewed video and its metadata to the YouTube or Instagram account you selected.

Each service processes that data under its own privacy policy. Remove a connection in Viral Hit and revoke access with the provider to stop future requests.

## Project files and deletion

Exported project files contain original media, Canvas and timelines, and exclude credentials, account connections and running jobs. Deleting a project or the data directory removes it from your installation; data already sent to a provider must be deleted with that provider.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change as the project evolves. Questions go to the [Viral Hit repository](https://github.com/slit-company/viral-hit/issues).
