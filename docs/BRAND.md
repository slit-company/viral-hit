Final prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished standalone brand key visual, landscape 1536x1024.
Primary request: Make the name "Viral Hit" itself look exceptionally clean, striking, fashionable and memorable for an independent open-source creative studio.
Scene/backdrop: bright warm-white matte background, airy premium editorial poster.
Subject: exact words "Viral Hit" on one line as the sole text, very large custom near-black bold forward-slanted grotesk lettering. Precision diagonal cuts, beautifully controlled kerning, clearly readable at thumbnail size. A small vivid acid-lime #caff51 fragment at the upper right of the capital H, and a distinctive crisp diagonal cut in the V. Preserve the correctly spelled words V-i-r-a-l H-i-t.
Composition: central dominant wordmark occupying roughly 80% of width; generous negative space all around; completely uncropped. One elegant oversize acid-lime elliptical motion stroke sweeps behind the typography on a shallow diagonal, with generous whitespace between the stroke and image edges. The single stroke gives motion and a rebellious creative-software attitude, never obscuring letterforms. Restrained near-black, acid-lime and warm-white only.
Style: impeccable flat graphic design, crisp print-quality edges, contemporary editorial identity with streetwear confidence. Optical balance matters; lettering is the hero.
Constraints: no additional words, no slogans, no small print, no mockup objects, no interface, no photos, no comic characters, no brand comparisons, no badges, no shadows, no 3D, no gradient, no glow, no distressed texture. Original independent identity. Bright background, not dark.
```

Visual review checked spelling, full lettering bounds, contrast and spacing at the native size. Local evidence is under `research/evidence/brand-key-visual/`. This review is a design judgment, not evidence of user preference or marketing performance.

## Final generation prompt

### Typographic wordmark (built-in image_gen)

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished typographic wordmark image for the creative software brand "Viral Hit".
Create one exceptionally polished custom wordmark, exact text "Viral Hit" in this capitalization, no other text. Make the lettering itself the logo: very bold, slightly forward-leaning contemporary grotesk with distinctive precision-cut terminals, tight but exceptionally well judged kerning, a confident editorial streetwear-meets-professional-creative-tool attitude. A subtle diagonal cut in the capital V and one small acid-lime #caff51 fragment at the upper right of the H can imply an editing cut and momentum, but every letter must remain immediately readable. Large near-black #161616 lettering, flat solid color. Clean silhouette with no fine details so it reads beautifully in an app header. One line, horizontal composition about 4:1, generous transparent margin around all edges, no cropping. Actual transparent alpha background. No separate icon, no slogan, no underline, no enclosing box, no 3D, no bevels, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no glow.Exact readable lettering is essential.

### App symbol (built-in image_gen)

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final app symbol for Viral Hit, an open-source AI video creation and editing studio. Create ONE exceptionally clean original logo mark, without any lettering: a bold geometric V made from two broad asymmetric forward-slanted ribbons, with a small sharply cut negative-space notch suggesting a video cut and forward momentum. Confident, playful, fast, memorable, premium creative software identity; clean flat vector-like edges, no decorative complexity. Palette: solid near-black ink #161616 as the main shape, one small acid-lime #caff51 cut-tip accent integrated into the mark. Square composition with the symbol centered occupying 82% of canvas, optically balanced and highly legible at 24px and 32px. Background fully transparent with actual alpha. No words, no initials H, no play-button triangle, no lightning bolt, no four-point AI sparkle, no gradients, no 3D, no shadow, no mockup, no border or containing square.
